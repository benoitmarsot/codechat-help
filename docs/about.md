# About LiquidAmber

LiquidAmber builds AI systems for serious research. The platform helps organizations turn years of documents, reporting, records, and institutional knowledge into answers that are accessible, verifiable, and useful.

It is designed for knowledge-intensive work, including investigations, policy analysis, academic research, compliance, strategy, intelligence, and content development.

## Our mission

An organization's most valuable knowledge often lives across disconnected documents, past decisions, and the experience of its people. That knowledge becomes difficult to use when links break, systems change, or experienced team members leave.

LiquidAmber's mission is to make institutional knowledge immediately accessible, verifiable, and actionable. Researchers can ask questions in natural language, follow the evidence behind an answer, and build new work on approved historical and current information.

## Built for trustworthy research

LiquidAmber is designed around control and traceability:

- **Source-grounded answers** connect claims to the documents and links behind them.
- **Approved knowledge** keeps research within the sources an organization chooses.
- **Access-aware retrieval** respects permissions, subscriptions, entitlements, and content policies.
- **Data control** means customer content is not used to train models.
- **Audit-ready output** preserves the research trail for professional review.
- **Flexible integration** works with existing content management systems, digital asset managers, databases, and document stores.
- **Model independence** lets organizations choose language models without tying the platform to one provider.

## The product ecosystem

LiquidAmber products support the path from research to public understanding:

| Product | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [Discover](discover.md) | Searches archives and connected sources, then returns cited answers for researchers, editors, and reporters. |
| [Describe](describe.md) | Turns findings and structured data into charts, timelines, and publication-ready visual stories. |
| [Verify](verify.md) | Reviews accuracy, evidence, ethics, style, and search readiness before publication. |
| [Engage](engage.md) | Gives audiences conversational access to trusted published content through a cited, access-aware experience. |

Together, these tools help teams find prior knowledge, explain what it means, review it carefully, and make it useful to their audiences.
